# Privacy Policy — VIA7012 Doctoral Copilot

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

VIA7012 Doctoral Copilot v1.0 is a skills-only academic plugin. It does not operate an external server, connect to an external database, or independently collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information.

The plugin processes only information the user provides in the ChatGPT conversation and files intentionally made available to the conversation, subject to applicable OpenAI product and account policies.

Version 1.0 has no independent backend, analytics database, tracking system, advertising system, or third-party data broker integration.

Users should avoid including unnecessary personally identifiable student information, confidential institutional data, protected educational records, passwords, or other secrets.

If a future version adds external services, MCP servers, LMS integrations, databases, or analytics, this policy will be updated before those capabilities are publicly released.

For questions, use the support route listed in the plugin repository.
