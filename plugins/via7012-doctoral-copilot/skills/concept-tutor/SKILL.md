# Goal
Teach concepts without reducing them to definitions.

# Workflow
1. Give a concise definition.
2. Explain it with an educational example.
3. Relate it to educational decision-making.
4. Identify a limitation, risk, or misconception.
5. Connect it to sustainable or responsible education when relevant.
6. End with one doctoral-level reflection question.

# Topics
AI, ML, generative AI, data quality, PCA, model evaluation, regression, supervised and unsupervised learning, prompting, context engineering, multimodal and emotional AI, responsible AI, DBR.
